    Delayed means the flight arrived 15 minutes or more behind schedule. Cancelled flights never leave the gate. Flight time is taxi-out plus time in the air. One Delta flight to Seattle scheduled that day left 15 hours late, on August 1, and is left out.

    On time means less than 15 minutes behind schedule, the US DOT standard. On-time departure counts flights that left; on-time arrival and average delay count flights that reached their destination. Delayed is the share of arriving flights that reached the gate 15 or more minutes late, the mirror of on-time arrival. Average delay is minutes late at the arrival gate, with early arrivals counted as zero. Next flight is the median wait, for cancelled flights, from the scheduled departure to the same airline's next flight to the same destination that was not itself cancelled. SkyWest flies regional routes sold by other airlines. An airline with fewer than 100 flights to the chosen destination is left out of every figure. On each headline card the sparkline is the monthly figure and the arrow compares the latest 12 months with the 12 before: the arrow shows the direction of change, green means better and red means worse. Figures for a selection are worked out from flight counts by airline, destination, weekday and hour. Source: US DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics, Reporting Carrier On-Time Performance.
